# Audience adaptation and bilingual review

## Lesson guide
Use source-backed learning goals, learner instructions, deliverables, success criteria and accessible resources. Rephrase teacher procedure in the second person without inventing new activities. An assignment excerpt may be a guide within a course: say that explicitly rather than claiming to export the entire course. Historical imports are not evidence of current delivery; show a source-era or review label, and do not invent dates.

Remove teacher instructions, answers, diagnostic student rosters, private interventions, dashboard URLs, IDs and unarchived private attachments. Preserve source requirements including duration, language restrictions, AI-use policy, numerical minimums and what is actually submitted. A checklist extracted from requirements is not an invented scoring rubric. Supplementary safety advice is allowed only if identified as editorial guidance, not original assessed requirements.

## Meeting brief
Separate agreed decisions, proposed changes, commitments, targets and unknowns. Action owners and deadlines require source evidence. Reconcile summaries with nearby transcript evidence when policy strength or attribution is inconsistent; keep uncertainty visible rather than selecting a convenient interpretation. Preserve 'preferred' versus 'required'. Relative meeting dates are not a license to fabricate dates if the source is contradictory.

Retain necessary colleague action owners for internal recipients. In general meeting briefs, omit unrelated student/parent names, performance judgments, jokes, confidential staffing/admissions cases, private access details and irrelevant strategy. For an explicitly authorized personnel-review brief, retain the relevant subject's name, supervisory evidence and performance judgments because these are the document's purpose; omit unrelated student identities and personnel details. Make it standalone with subject/date/evaluator metadata, not a recipient-role list or assistant preparation commentary. Do not treat source-summary arithmetic claims as authoritative: restate unresolved weights without endorsing an unverified sum. A meeting brief is an internal draft unless approved for a wider audience.

## Bilingual review checklist
- All labels, metadata values, navigation, table headers/cells, links, notices and controls exist in zh/en.
- Numbers, dates, source precision, name spelling, links, obligation strength and tentative status agree.
- Keep institutional acronyms such as PA/MC/H/CL/UIS unchanged unless their expansion is source-confirmed.
- Chinese names are not invented for English-only colleague names.
- Use readable natural translations, not sentence-order mirroring. No claims of official translation.

## Private evidence report
Outside the shareable package, record exact source paths and sections/line ranges, selection rationale, omitted sensitive categories, unresolved policy wording, and semantic checks. Do not put raw notes or review provenance into hidden HTML fields.
